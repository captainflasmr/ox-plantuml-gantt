* Notes
- Parent headlines (headlines with children) become section separators.
- Leaf headlines with SCHEDULED, DEADLINE, or :BLOCKER: become tasks.
- When both a date and :BLOCKER: are set, the date takes precedence and
  the dependency is not rendered.

  line (as Org itself inserts them via C-c C-s / C-c C-d).
